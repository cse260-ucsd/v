How to use

  • Pick a loop Click a line of code. When the code has focus, ↑ and ↓ move between loops.
  • Step through it ← and → (or the arrow buttons) step the selected loop in the order the loop nest runs it. Stepping past its last iteration moves the loop above it along. Play steps automatically.
  • Jump to a block Click any block in a matrix.

The figure keeps every block big enough to see. Real blocks are far more lopsided: the L3 block is often several thousand elements on its long side, while the mr × nr tile is something like 6 × 8.
